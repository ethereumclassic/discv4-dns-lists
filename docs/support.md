# Support this work

These discovery trees are one of the public goods maintained in the
[`ethereumclassic`](https://github.com/ethereumclassic) organization, alongside
the [Core-Geth](https://github.com/ethereumclassic/core-geth) client and the
network's bootnodes. This page says who maintains and funds them, why they need
sponsors, and how a sponsor is credited. It is the one place this repository
publishes its funding routes. If you find them anywhere else, check them against
this page first.

## Who maintains them

The [Ethereum Classic DAO](https://ethereumclassicdao.org), a Wyoming DAO LLC, is
the legal entity that maintains these public goods, together with Ethereum
Classic's core developers and community contributors. The work is done in the
`ethereumclassic` organization, where each repository has more than one admin and
more than one maintainer. The repositories stay there no matter who maintains or
funds them.

That arrangement is new. Until now, this work moved between organizations as each
one closed or left: ETCDEV in 2018, ETC Labs in 2021, and the ETC Cooperative,
which entered maintenance mode at the end of 2024 and is due to dissolve.
Along the way, the client changed repositories, the bootnodes and
discovery trees changed operators, and Core-Geth went 21 months without security
maintenance. A standing legal entity, with the software kept in the community's
own organization, is meant to end that churn.

With the ETC Cooperative's dissolution, Ethereum Classic stakeholders such as
mining pools, exchanges and service providers should use
<security@ethereumclassic.net> as their point of contact. A person answers it:
one of the core developers who maintain this work. Sponsorship has its own
address, given below.

on top of maintaining it.

**Sponsors are credited here.** A sponsoring organization is added to this table
with its name, a link and the years it has contributed, and can be credited for
the specific work it funds. A sponsor that prefers not to be named is not listed.

## Why sponsor

If you are a stakeholder of Ethereum Classic, you rely on this infrastructure.
Mining pools, centralized exchanges and custodians, issuers of Ethereum Classic
financial products, mining hardware manufacturers, block explorers and RPC
providers all depend on a client that follows the network and on peer discovery
that works every night. **Ethereum Classic has no protocol-layer funding
mechanism for its critical infrastructure or its core developers**, so no part of
the network's fee or issuance revenue pays for this work.

The organization that maintains it is in place. What it needs is steady funding
from the stakeholders who depend on it, so that the work no longer rests on a
single backer.

## How to sponsor

**An invoiced agreement, for organizations.** If your business depends on this
infrastructure, a maintenance sponsorship can be invoiced against a purchase
order like any other vendor relationship, which is usually what a finance
department needs. Write to <donations@ethereumclassic.net> with what you run and
the period you want to cover.

**A direct transfer.** For anyone who prefers to send funds directly, an address
is provided on request. Write to <donations@ethereumclassic.net>. If a large
transfer is planned, send a small one first and confirm receipt at the same
address.

**This project does not publish a donation address.** An address shown in a
screenshot, a fork, a mirror or a message did not come from this project unless
it arrived in a reply from <donations@ethereumclassic.net>.

Funding mail goes to `ethereumclassic.net`, the domain for the public goods
maintained in the `ethereumclassic` organization.

## What sponsorship pays for

For these trees: running the pipeline that crawls the network and publishes
them every night, keeping the three domains registered and their DNS served,
answering a refused run on the
[status issue](https://github.com/ethereumclassic/discv4-dns-lists/issues?q=label%3Apipeline-status),
and updating the fork pins when the network passes a fork.
[Core-Geth's support page](https://docs.coregeth.com/support/) describes what it
pays for in the client.

## What sponsorship does not buy

**No place in the trees.** A node is listed on
[the same criteria](node-selection.md#what-gets-a-node-listed) as every other,
whoever runs it and whoever sponsors this work, and every list published is
committed here, where anyone can check it. Being credited as a sponsor records a
contribution. It is not an endorsement of the sponsor's products or services.

Requests and reports are welcome from anyone, sponsor or not, in
[this repository's issues](https://github.com/ethereumclassic/discv4-dns-lists/issues).
